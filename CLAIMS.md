# PEAD-Bench Claim Ledger

Ledger version: 1.0
Study ID: `PEAD-MAIN-v1`
Paper boundary: fixed MAVS-GC and Diagnostic Sciences only

## Scientific identity

PEAD-Bench tests an authorization function over a declared evidence state. Predictive support and authorization are separate computations:

> Prediction estimates support. Governance determines whether the available evidence authorizes action.

H1 and H2 are independently reportable. H1 can pass while H2 fails. No MAVS result may repair a failed or invalid H1 evaluation.

## Hypotheses and nulls

### H1 - Information necessity

Prediction-facing state alone is insufficient to determine `Accept`, `Reject`, or `Escalate` on the registered authorization-divergent PEAD class.

Null `N1`: P-only methods match Raw-G methods on verified divergent classes without privileged information or excessive rejection/escalation.

Null `N3`: the effect is an exact-equality artifact that disappears under near-equivalence, reversal sequences, or structural/domain holdouts.

### H2 - Architecture value

Under matched raw governance information, cases, budgets, tuning, and reporting, structured MAVS-GC provides a pre-registered protected, scope, or transfer advantage over the strongest eligible flat/generic Raw-G comparator.

Null `N2`: strong flat Raw-G methods match or exceed MAVS-GC on transfer, composition, scope, ambiguity, and protected utility.

Null `N4`: the generator or label engines encode MAVS-specific assumptions.

Null `N5`: apparent safety is fully explained by rejection or escalation collapse.

### H3 - Adaptive perception

Self-Learning MAVS is outside Paper 1. It may be studied only in a separately versioned companion experiment. H3 is not required to validate PEAD or fixed MAVS-GC.

## Frozen claims

### C1 - Existence

Authorization-divergent predictive-equivalence classes exist in the registered causal grammar.

- Required metrics: `PEI`, `ADI`, dual-label agreement.
- Required splits: exact development fixtures and final exact structural/domain banks.
- Required audits: equivalence, authorization, generator independence, trace completeness.
- Eligible only if: exact pairs are byte-identical after canonicalization, divergent labels are independently verified, and integrity gates pass.
- Forbidden wording: universal existence across arbitrary real-world distributions.

### C2 - Prediction-only lower bound

Any P-only terminal rule incurs error or deferral on balanced authorization-divergent classes.

- Required metrics: pair-level lower bound, terminal error, coverage, escalation, UAR, FRR.
- Required splits: exact and near blind banks with matched controls.
- Required audits: lower-bound proof, equivalence, balance, leakage, access, abstention.
- Eligible only if: the empirical P-only frontier matches the registered bound and no predictive leakage is detected.
- Forbidden wording: every predictive system is insufficient in every environment.

### C3 - Governance information

Raw governance evidence permits escape from the P-only frontier.

- Required metrics: governance information gain, protected frontier change, coverage-matched UAR/FRR.
- Required splits: matched P-only and Raw-G cases across exact, near, reversal, structural, and domain banks.
- Required audits: cross-profile ID equality, access parity, budget parity, contamination.
- Eligible only if: Raw-G escapes the P-only frontier on claim-bearing blind families.
- Forbidden wording: structured MAVS is necessary or optimal.

### C4 - Structured governance

MAVS-GC uses the same Raw-G evidence more robustly than eligible flat/generic Raw-G competitors under selected held-out conditions.

- Required metrics: governance architecture gain, scope leakage, transfer, worst-world loss, ambiguity calibration, invariant fidelity.
- Required splits: matched Raw-G structural and domain holdouts.
- Required audits: equal information, matched budget, method fidelity, scalar compression, ablations, contamination.
- Eligible only if: at least one frozen architecture-specific advantage survives the relevant holdout and robust operating neighborhood.
- Forbidden wording: universal MAVS superiority, official reproduction of proxy comparators, or equality of human design effort.

### C5 - Diagnostic Sciences

Diagnostic scope, authority, interaction, and evidence availability are causal governance properties.

- Required metrics: sensitivity, specificity, conditional predictive effect, in-scope influence, out-of-scope influence, redundancy, instability, composition, leakage, discontinuity.
- Required splits: positive, matched-negative, boundary, and adversarial out-of-scope banks for every diagnostic.
- Required audits: scope-contract schema, authority-path, composition, nuisance invariance, diagnostic ablation.
- Eligible only if: registered diagnostics add in-scope value without prohibited out-of-scope authority.
- Forbidden wording: every diagnostic is universally valid or raw correlation is independently harmful.

### C6 - Evidence boundary

When authorization-relevant distinctions are unavailable under permitted evidence, calibrated escalation is preferable to forced certainty.

- Required metrics: ambiguity accuracy, forced-certainty error, escalation calibration, unnecessary escalation, resolution value.
- Required splits: resolvable, reducibly ambiguous, and irreducibly ambiguous evidence banks.
- Required audits: complete ambiguity certificates, evidence access, label agreement, abstention degeneracy.
- Eligible only if: ambiguity truth is completely certified and escalation improves the registered protected frontier.
- Forbidden wording: escalation alone is safety or non-coverage is success.

## Non-claims

PEAD-Bench does not claim:

- every existing AI method lacks governance;
- MAVS-GC is universally optimal;
- deployment readiness, a certification standard, or zero real-world risk;
- zero observed unsafe acceptance certifies zero risk;
- escalation or rejection alone constitutes safety;
- an adaptation or proxy is an official external framework evaluation;
- Paper 1 depends on Self-Learning MAVS;
- negative results or failed MAVS cases may be omitted.

## Outcome tiers

| Tier | Objective condition | Maximum permitted interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Integrity-valid negative | All integrity gates pass; H1 fails | The registered PEAD formulation did not establish prediction insufficiency |
| Paradigm support | Exact/near P-only frontier matches the lower bound and Raw-G escapes on blind families | Prediction-facing state is insufficient on the registered PEAD class |
| Generalized paradigm support | Paradigm effect persists across structural and domain holdouts | The effect covers the broad registered class, not one grammar |
| Architectural support | MAVS improves a frozen protected/scope/transfer frontier over the best equal-information Raw-G comparator | Structured governance adds value in the registered conditions |
| Strongest result | Generalized H1, architectural support, scalar-compression failure, and audit-perfect reproduction | Paradigm-level impact potential; not universal validity, deployment readiness, or zero risk |

## Negative-result publication policy

Integrity gates decide whether a result is valid. Performance decides only which bounded conclusion is eligible.

- H1 failure with intact integrity is published as an integrity-valid negative result.
- H1 success with H2 failure supports only governance-information necessity on the registered class.
- Scientific underperformance never permits redesign, retuning, omission, or selective rerun.
- Infrastructure failure invalidates affected artifacts and requires documented repair and regeneration.

## Mandatory stop conditions

Execution stops for:

1. a released exact pair with non-identical `PredictiveState`;
2. a released dual-label disagreement;
3. method access to a hidden label or forbidden field;
4. final-bank contamination or post-freeze tuning;
5. missing headline trace lineage;
6. a clean-reproduction failure that changes a claim;
7. evidence that truth generation depends on MAVS code.
